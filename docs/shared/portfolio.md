# Turn study into employable evidence

Build a small, honest portfolio of working systems and decisions.

## Learn

A certification demonstrates a defined assessment outcome, not automatic job readiness. Employers also look for communication, troubleshooting, practical judgment and evidence that you can finish work.

Use a portfolio project with requirements, architecture, tests, security/cost choices and a recovery story. Prefer a modest working system to a huge unverified feature list. Explain trade-offs and limitations.

Never publish real credentials, customer data or confidential screenshots. Use synthetic data and document the scope of any lab. Keep an evidence journal of failures and what you learned.

## Worked example

A support portfolio can contain a home-lab inventory, three well-written incident tickets and a tested restore. A cloud portfolio can add infrastructure code, a cost estimate and teardown evidence without claiming production experience.

## Practice safely

1. Choose one job family and one small project.
2. Write acceptance criteria before building.
3. Record tests and a failure/recovery exercise.
4. Prepare a five-minute explanation of your decisions.

## Check your understanding

Which portfolio statement is most trustworthy?

- “Enterprise-ready” with no tests
- A precise tested scope with known limitations
- A copied certificate image
- A list of tools never used

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

A precise tested scope with known limitations. Honest evidence lets an employer assess real capability.

</details>
