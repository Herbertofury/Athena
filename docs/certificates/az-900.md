## Guided capstone

### Map Azure services to requirements

1. Map a fictional organization into management groups, subscriptions and resource groups.
2. Classify proposed services by service model and explain shared responsibility.
3. Distinguish Entra identity, Azure RBAC, Policy, locks and cost management with one example each.
4. Produce a non-deployed resource and cost-governance plan.

### Acceptance evidence

- Accurate governance hierarchy.
- Five clearly distinguished management controls.
- Dated official references and explicit non-deployment status.
