## Guided capstone

### Explain a cloud solution and its bill

1. Choose a fictional small business workload and list its business requirements.
2. Compare a VM, managed application and SaaS approach using the shared-responsibility model.
3. Use the official pricing calculator without deploying anything.
4. Explain service selection, identity ownership, support needs and variable cost drivers in a one-page brief.

### Acceptance evidence

- A responsibility matrix with customer obligations.
- A dated estimate with assumptions, not a promised bill.
- Clear distinction between foundational knowledge and architecture implementation.
