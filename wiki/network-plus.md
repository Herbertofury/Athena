## Guided capstone

### Design and diagnose a small network

1. Design two private IPv4 subnets for fictional departments with stated host counts.
2. Calculate network, broadcast, usable range and gateways manually, then check your results.
3. Draw switching, routing, DHCP and DNS roles and permitted application flows.
4. Use a local simulator or paper packet walk to diagnose an incorrect prefix and an incorrect resolver.

### Acceptance evidence

- Address plan with no overlap and enough usable addresses.
- Layer-by-layer explanation of two failures.
- A monitoring and change/rollback plan.
