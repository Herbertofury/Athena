## Guided capstone

### Build a routed lab network

1. Use an authorized network simulator or owned lab devices; record software and device versions.
2. Build two VLANs with a routed path and a documented IPv4 address plan.
3. Verify same-VLAN and cross-VLAN reachability, then deliberately introduce one selector/addressing error.
4. Capture the relevant interface, VLAN and route evidence; repair and retest.
5. Explain which v1.1 objectives this small lab exercises and which remain untouched.

### Acceptance evidence

- Topology and versioned configuration files.
- Before/after reachability and route evidence.
- No claim that one lab covers OSPF, IPv6, wireless, automation or the entire blueprint.
