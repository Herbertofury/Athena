# Afdian adapter research

[Adapter laboratory](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Athena/wiki/Engineering-Site-Adapters) · Reviewed October 26, 2026

## Current route

The documented creator API covers orders and supporters with signed requests; it is not evidence of a universal paid-post reader. Keep public-post extraction separate from authorized content.

## Reuse before replacement

Reuse exact-post identity and media-role tests. Compare complete owned galleries, not generic first-image heuristics.

## Acceptance workload

Opaque post IDs; avatar exclusion; original/preview distinction; posters versus streams; duplicates; access expiry; pagination.

## Evidence status

Developer documentation reviewed; no authenticated post-media acceptance. No recommendation transfers to a project until it beats that project's actual baseline without sacrificing requirements.
