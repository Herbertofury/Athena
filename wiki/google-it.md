## Guided capstone

### Support desk incident portfolio

1. Create three fictional support tickets: a name-resolution failure, a storage-capacity warning and an application permission error.
2. For each ticket record symptoms, scope, three hypotheses and one low-risk discriminating test.
3. Perform one harmless local test on your own device and record its actual output.
4. Write a plain-language user update and an escalation package that separates observation from inference.

### Acceptance evidence

- Three complete tickets with no passwords or personal data.
- At least one actual test output, with platform and timestamp.
- Each conclusion is supported by evidence or explicitly left uncertain.
