## Guided capstone

### Threat model and incident tabletop

1. Choose a fictional three-tier service and classify its data and trust boundaries.
2. Write five threat scenarios, their impact, likely weaknesses and proposed controls.
3. Create a tabletop incident with a timeline and explicit containment/recovery criteria.
4. Test the plan with a peer or by walking every dependency and record unanswered questions.

### Acceptance evidence

- Threat model, risk register and residual-risk owners.
- Evidence-handling and communication plan.
- Recovery validation that does not destroy investigation evidence.
