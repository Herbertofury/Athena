# Game coverage roadmap

The schema is multi-game; this list records requested scope, not populated databases or verified provider support.

- The Sims 4
- The Elder Scrolls: Arena, Daggerfall, Morrowind, Oblivion, Oblivion Remastered, Skyrim (original), Skyrim Special Edition and Anniversary Edition runtimes; The Elder Scrolls Online, Legends, Blades, Castles, Travels and future franchise releases are separate discovery tracks, not assumed to support equivalent modding.
- Fallout 4 and Starfield
- Minecraft: Java/Bedrock and loader/runtime distinctions
- Hytale and Cyberpunk 2077
- Other games recovered from the user's existing projects after inventory review

Use distinct game/edition IDs, explicit release/runtime/build and platform. Do not treat Oblivion Remastered as interchangeable with original Oblivion, or Skyrim runtime updates as universally compatible. Map provider-specific game IDs explicitly. Future or unsupported games remain unknown, not fabricated records.

Every game needs its own creator/source catalog, tools, best practices, how-tos, troubleshooting and scoped compatibility/warning coverage. Shared schema does not imply identical mod formats, APIs, permissions or tested behavior.
