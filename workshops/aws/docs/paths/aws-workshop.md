# AWS Foundations: Evidence Before Architecture

Ten substantial lessons connect AWS service concepts to bounded local reasoning models, explicit evidence and honest gaps.

## Prerequisites

cloud-model-contract, cloud-responsibility-scope, aws-design

## Lessons

1. [Trace AWS identity decisions without inventing an IAM engine](../shared/awsf-identity.md)
2. [Explain VPC reachability as several independent conditions](../shared/awsf-network.md)
3. [Choose storage by interface, access pattern and recovery need](../shared/awsf-storage.md)
4. [Fit compute capacity to demand and failure assumptions](../shared/awsf-compute.md)
5. [Make duplicate delivery safe at the business boundary](../shared/awsf-queues.md)
6. [Separate relational failover, read scaling and access patterns](../shared/awsf-database.md)
7. [Test failure scope and recovery objectives independently](../shared/awsf-resilience.md)
8. [Match an incident question to the right operational evidence](../shared/awsf-operations.md)
9. [Reconcile synthetic costs without pretending a budget is a cap](../shared/awsf-cost.md)
10. [Defend an AWS-shaped design with evidence and unresolved risks](../shared/awsf-capstone.md)

## Work safely

Use the [workbook](../WORKBOOK.md) and [lab guide](../../labs/aws-workshop/README.md). Complete [coverage and gaps](../AWS-COVERAGE.md) before interpreting completion.

No AWS APIs, console, CLI, SDK, credentials, network, provisioning, CloudFormation, Terraform or paid services are used. Local models are not AWS engines or real configurations. Partial conceptual coverage is not exam readiness or deployment approval.
