## Work safely

Use the [workbook](../WORKBOOK.md) and [lab guide](../../labs/azure-workshop/README.md). Complete [coverage and gaps](../AZURE-COVERAGE.md) before interpreting completion.

No Azure CLI, SDK, APIs, credentials, network access, provisioning or paid services are used. All real Azure operations are unexecuted. Partial AZ-900 concepts and broader administrative enrichment are not full AZ-900/AZ-104 coverage or practical readiness. AZ-104 effective-date verification remains incomplete because retrieved official material conflicted.
