ATHENA / GAME SYSTEMS

Engine Foundry Play Lab

Three standalone game systems, running in Athena. No account, internet or installs. Survivor Arena and Warden Duel keep nothing unless you choose to download a local replay.

Survivor Arena

Play the original five-minute Classic, run a Marathon, mine moving geodes and chase goblins, discover optional side rifts, or spend collected loot at a camp workshop. In the 30-minute Frontier Expedition, take a route through a quiet outcrop or step into an ongoing faction battle. Ashglass 60, Floodplain 90 and Relay Endless add the cavern, ferry escort and farther outposts. Pause and resume from a local run tape.

Midnight City Walk

Explore the original geometry of five districts and enter four City interiors. Practice from a doorway or find one as you walk.

Warden Duel

Play a tactical practice deck with the original battle rules, then download or resume a verified card replay.

These are playable engine previews, not the original mascot art, 3D renderer, or full shipped games.
