## Review conclusions

The first write follows a deferred BEGIN with no intervening read, so this is not
a demonstrated read-to-write upgrade deadlock. BEGIN IMMEDIATE alone merely moves
lock admission to BEGIN; it does not guarantee FIFO admission. Increasing timeout,
relaxing EXTRA, silently replaying writes or reducing the64-record assertion would
not establish a correction.

The candidate explicitly queues same-process SnapshotStore writes before SQL and
releases admission after commit/rollback/unwind. FIFO reentry prevents one cooperating
writer repeatedly winning admission ahead of its waiting peers. Admission has its
own5s budget and256-waiter limit; SQLite retains its separate5s busy timeout. This
can add waiting compared with the old path and is not a5s end-to-end latency claim.
Other SQLite components/processes and non-Unix path aliases remain outside this
fairness contract. No general throughput, latency or root-cause proof is claimed.
