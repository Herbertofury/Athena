# Asset validation receipt

- Evidence level and exact asset/target scope:
- Original source/export revision, asset licenses and output hashes:
- Validator version, artifact integrity and capability list:
- Importer/renderer/game versions, separately from the validator:
- All input documents, buffers, images and authorized external resources:
- Callback/resource requests, supplied bytes and missing or unexamined inputs:
- Full issues, severities, options, suppression/override settings and truncation state:
- Required versus optional extensions and each component's tested support:
- Predeclared geometry, hierarchy, animation, material, skin/morph, metadata and quality invariants:
- Transformation baseline and equivalent-work comparison:
- Import, playback, interruption, rendering, lighting/color and device checks actually run:
- Native gameplay/networking/reload/save checks when relevant:
- Preserved failed expectations, incomplete reports and fresh reruns:
- Remaining acceptance gates and evidence that could change the decision:

A resolved validation call, zero errors or a smaller asset is not proof of retained intent or native compatibility. Publish only authorized assets and sanitized reports.
