## High-performance architecture to challenge

- Native streaming transfer and an exact provider identity contract first; compare downloader engines independently of crawl/extraction.
- Cache response validators and immutable content IDs; separate fresh observations, unchanged data, and failed refresh. Reuse connections and cap per-host concurrency according to documented limits.
- Direct documented API or HTTP/HTML before optional isolated JavaScript rendering. Separate first correct result, complete enrichment and render time. Avoid repeating download/decode/extract work per consumer; preserve complete results.
- A common versioned fixture contract should cover pagination, nested media, duplicate identities, empty/partial states, malformed responses, redirects, rate limits, interrupted transfers, changed validators, corrupt files and restart. Use local or authorized representative fixtures, not uncontrolled third-party load tests.
- Compare with Enderloom, Modhunter and GameSync's actual current implementations; adopt only compatible no-loss improvements. No universal winner or new benchmark claim. CLI-first, project-local state, permissions and distribution rights still apply.
