# Roguelike & Card-Game Radar — 2026-11-25

## Exceptional mechanics

### Handmancers 1.0 — ADAPT
Official: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2423290/Handmancers/ . Released 2026-11-12. Rock/Paper/Scissors forms a compact tactical grammar, deepened by up to three modifiers per card and artifacts. Powerful plays accumulate Cramps that constrain later options until recovery. The 1.0 update adds new class/content, Blight, Brittle, Combo and Muster effects, expanded Corruption Delving and rebuilt meta progression. Steam reports 88% Very Positive from 84 purchaser reviews and Steam Deck Verified; mouse-only/touch-only and pace-friendly options are listed. Proprietary game: no code/art/text/card redistribution rights. Independently adapt the pattern of burst benefit -> accumulated constraint -> recovery choice. Candidate consumers: JetSetCraft Rival Rush (limited run-local overexertion for high-impact upgrades) and Modhunter Siege (hero-specific strain/recovery); do not impose fatigue on every action. Preserve mode identities. Source: https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appgroupname=Activision&enddate=1794504450&feed=steam_community_announcements

### Hadean Tactics — ADAPT ARCHITECTURE
Developer: https://emberfishgames.com/ . Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1020820/Hadean_Tactics/ . Its 2.0/Moonhunter update (2026-04-29) adds Eternal Rift, Moon Phases/Eclipse, cards, units, relics, alliances and events; Switch release is 2026-11-25. Recruited units fight autonomously in real time while cards influence their behavior: cards act as command/behavior modifiers, not only direct attacks. Developer reports 100,000+ Steam copies and an 89% Very Positive rating; not independently audited. Proprietary game and assets; no reuse rights. Candidate consumers: JetSetCraft summons/companions in a separate mode, Modhunter Siege squad orders/intent, CrownFold autonomous formations. Prototype only a narrow card-to-allowlisted-unit-order adapter with deterministic event logs; do not transplant the autobattler. Source: https://www.gamespress.com/Acclaimed-Roguelike-Deckbuilder-Hadean-Tactics-Launches-Today-on-Ninte

### 9 Kings 1.0 — ADAPT STRATEGIC MODIFIERS
Official: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2784470/9_Kings/ . Released 2026-11-12. Its 1.0 announcement describes singleplayer, multiplayer, quest and Joker custom-rules modes plus 130+ cards; Patch #33 (Nov 18) adds a new season and victory tracks. The interesting pattern is player-defined rules layered over kingdom/army composition and authored quest constraints. Steam reports 87% Very Positive recent reviews (834) and 92% overall English reviews (10,714), plus Steam Deck Verified. Proprietary; no code/assets reuse. Candidate consumers: Modhunter Atlas Campaign and Shiverfell for authored challenge mutators/escalating siege compositions; CrownFold for faction doctrines and constrained scenarios. Share schemas, not content or balance. Source: https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?enddate=1789563638&feed=steam_community_announcements

## Reusable implementation candidates

### Cantrip — RESEARCH ONLY
Repo: https://github.com/AGomnes/Cantrip . Pinned revision: 974a915ef0c3c6bb3045c9c59b0bf9493fdfb8ae; 1.0.1; MIT. Declarative text-authored cards/statuses/relics/enemies/heroes/abilities; before/instead/after event phases; stacking statuses, layered modifiers, targeting, enemy intents/phases, player choices, editor lint/tests, live card-text preview, import/export checks and causality trace. Cards and turns are optional, so the same model can express cooldown abilities. Requires Godot 4.6.x .NET, a C# solution and .NET SDK 8+, even if game logic is GDScript; matching Cantrip.Core package. Retain MIT notices. Not a drop-in for Java Forge or browser JS and not a complete proven deck/hand engine. Do not add it directly to JetSetCraft, Modhunter or CrownFold. Adapt the language-neutral ideas: typed effects, explicit trigger ordering, stack policy, content linting and event traces. Register under gameplay.typed-effect-resolution as a research candidate only. Source: https://godotassetlibrary.com/asset/chEKxy/cantrip-c

### roguelite-core — RESEARCH / FIXTURE COMPARISON
Repo: https://github.com/tiagomaciels/roguelite-core . Pinned revision: 65d68588c6f8576540fed7589fc6baa8ffa54e3f; package 0.1.0; MIT. Pure TypeScript deterministic content-agnostic auto-battler: seeded maps, battle resolution, elemental effectiveness, recruitment/trades, branching evolutions, team synergy traits, statuses, permadeath, immutable snapshots/reducers and batch simulation. Runtime is browser/worker/server/headless compatible; Node/TS toolchain for development. Small project, limited adoption, no production benchmarks or second-host acceptance. Compare reducers, fixtures and batch balance simulation with JetSetCraft Rival Rush, Modhunter Siege and CrownFold; browser JS consumers are more directly compatible than Java. Do not replace existing engines. Register as research_only under gameplay.seeded-run-state. Source: https://github.com/tiagomaciels/roguelite-core

### DelveRL — RESEARCH ONLY
Repo: https://github.com/SnyderConsulting/DelveRL . MIT; Godot 4.7+. Deterministic-after-reset, procedural, partially observed roguelike with renderer-independent environment API, batched 16-action training API, observation-only baseline and recurrent PPO trainer. Project-authored held-out results report 90.625% floor-10 clears across 32 episodes; extended 8-episode diagnostic reports 100% floor-10 clears, three runs still alive at 5,000 turns. Not independently replicated; repo has one commit and minimal community activity. Useful for hidden-information-safe agent observations and deterministic QA, not a gameplay dependency. Python/PyTorch trainer; MIT notices required. Source: https://github.com/SnyderConsulting/DelveRL

## Existing portfolio baseline and decisions
Athena's Game Systems Exchange already documents JetSetCraft Rival Rush seeded runs, six-room route, upgrade drafts, rerolls/banishes, evolutions and bounded arena contracts; Adventure Mode is distinct. Modhunter has Siege classes/upgrades/bosses, authored regional contracts, Atlas Campaign and Shiverfell; Chronicle has scoped events, idempotent purchases and save revisions. Game-only rewards must never mint verified MO2 Forge Sigils. CrownFold has research/technology cards, limited equip slots, synergies and deterministic RTS war simulation, not a full deckbuilder. No complete reusable deck/hand/discard/turn-resolution engine is verified.

Keep existing game identities and implementations. Prototype resource-pressure and command-card mechanics only as isolated host-native experiments. Cantrip is a research candidate, not a shared runtime. roguelite-core is a reducer/fixture comparison candidate, not an engine replacement. Keep gameplay.card-game-engine at comparison_needed until a real host proves card instances, deck/draw/hand/discard/exhaust/removed/resolving zones, phases, costs, targets, typed effects, deterministic logs, migrations and zone-conservation tests. Share contracts and fixtures first; promote code only after equivalent tests in two compatible hosts.
