# Roguelike & Card Game Radar — 2026-11-26

## Forge the Fates — ADAPT / isolated prototype, not production adoption

- Launch: 2026-11-25, Chaos Logic Studios / Surefire.Games. Official store and launch announcement: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3911490/Forge_the_Fates/ ; free demo: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3948880/ ; developer announcement preserved at https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/26893816/ (build 26893816).
- Mechanic: played cards place runes on a tactical grid; swapping and shifting enables match-three chains. Unmatched placements persist into subsequent turns, so players trade immediate output against a future board setup. Deck can be retuned before encounters. Full launch is new, not the invention of the mechanic: the demo already documented it in August 2025.
- Quality evidence as retrieved 2026-11-26: four positive full-game purchaser reviews, insufficient for a score; demo 11 positive reviews. Small samples only. Developer documents a playable seven-battle first act. No independent hands-on testing.
- Why prototype: persistent spatial state gives a second tactical axis to card selection and makes delayed combinations possible without merely increasing card damage. It can be an optional board-rules module rather than another engine.
- Runtime: Windows 10/11, dual-core 2 GHz, 4 GB RAM, 2 GB storage listed for full game; engine and source availability unknown. Proprietary commercial game; no verified permission to redistribute assets/code/text. Independently author mechanics, content and visuals. Steam lists mouse-only option, full controller support, adjustable difficulty, save-anytime, volume controls, no QTEs, Steam Deck Playable. No measured performance.
- Athena owner: [Card Game Engine](../../engines/catalog.json), [Engine Foundry](../../docs/ENGINE-FOUNDRY.md), [M5 #365](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Athena/issues/365). Existing GameSync 23-module rules platform is the intake baseline, not a missing-from-scratch engine; GameSync Next has 19/23 identical files. Extract and qualify core before installing optional rules in production. Existing candidate status remains comparison_needed; no new engine family.
- Contract proposal: optional board state with stable piece IDs, coordinates, deck-to-board zone transitions, legal moves, ordered match/cascade events and explicit persistence between turns; specify whether matched pieces leave the board or transform. Seed and log random refill where applicable; preview via pure reducer, commit against a revision, and keep presentation local. Bound resolution by documented game rules and reject cyclic effects rather than silently truncating valid outcomes. Test conservation, simultaneous-match ordering, replay, save/resume between turns and preview/resolution parity. Measure full-chain p95/p99 latency and memory on cold and warm equivalent inputs; avoid permanent polling and preserve all features. Provide keyboard/controller selection, non-color shape/icon labels, reduced-motion preview, undo-before-commit and textual chain explanation.
- Proposed consumer links (not accepted integrations): [GameSync](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Gamesync) and [GameSync Next](https://github.com/Herbertofury/GameSync-Next) first as optional puzzle/deck mode; [JetSetCraft Adventure Mode](https://github.com/Herbertofury/JetSetCraft) as optional traversal puzzle/shrine that returns a local reward; Rival Rush remains real-time arena, no mandatory board. [Modhunter Shiverfell/Atlas Campaign](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Modhunter) as optional ward/expedition puzzle only, Siege stays survivor-style and never mints verified Forge Sigils from game activity. [CrownFold](https://github.com/Herbertofury/CrownFold) may research persistent formation bonuses for an optional planning puzzle; retain RTS and technology loadouts, do not turn into match-three combat.
- Route via gameplay.card-game-engine, gameplay.card-run-bridge, gameplay.typed-effect-resolution and gameplay.game-research-radar. No upstream proprietary implementation candidate and no new runtime dependency. Prototype only after owner scope approval, then compare real hosts and keep canonical status unpromoted until two consumers pass.
